THE PERSON BEHIND SK AUTOHAUS

Meet Our Owner

SK AutoHaus is built on a genuine passion for automobiles, practical industry experience and a belief that quality automotive care comes down to attention to detail. That approach shapes how every vehicle is treated and how every customer is served.

The owner comes from the family business SK Tyres, Kollam, a background that has provided valuable experience and an understanding of the automotive industry, customer relationships and the importance of proper vehicle care.

With SK AutoHaus, that foundation is carried forward through a commitment to professional service, thoughtful vehicle care and consistently maintaining the standards customers expect when they entrust their vehicles to the team.
